\subsection{Measuring Sycophancy}
\label{sec:rw-sycophancy}

Sycophancy has been measured at two horizons. Single-turn protocols
score one response to one challenge: model-written evaluations show
answers shifting toward beliefs the user reveals
\citep{perez2022discovering}, controlled studies find the preference for
agreement generalizes across task types \citep{sharma2023sycophancy},
and diagnostic frameworks standardize it across domains
\citep{fanous2025syceval}. Fixed-horizon
multi-turn protocols extend this to a dialogue whose user turns are
written in advance: over multiple-choice items
\citep{liu2025truthdecay}, with a single follow-up challenge
\citep{laban2024flipflop}, or in free-form text across five scripted
turns \citep{SYCON}.
% [OPT-1] --- cut to reach the 500-word budget ---
The behavior also reproduces outside benchmark
settings, in escalatory multi-turn medical consultations
\citep{kim2026doctor} and in multi-agent debate, where inter-agent
sycophancy collapses deliberation into premature consensus
\citep{yao2025peacemaker}.
% [OPT-2] --- cut to reach the 500-word budget. Mirrors
% SYCON-Bench's own step 4: map the response literature even
% though this paper proposes no mitigation. ---
A parallel line proposes remedies, among them
pinpoint tuning \citep{chen2024yesmen}, linear-probe penalties
\citep{papadatos2024probe}, and synthetic-data interventions
\citep{wei2023synthetic}.
% [END OPT-1, OPT-2]

A mechanistic line of work indicates that sycophancy is not one
behavior. Sycophantic agreement and sycophantic praise occupy distinct
linear directions and can be amplified or suppressed independently
\citep{vennemeyer2025sycophancy}, factual and opinion subtypes
dissociate representationally \citep{baez2026dissociating}, and in
reasoning models the behavior is visible within the trace rather than
only in the final answer \citep{monica2026anonymous}. SPINE adopts the
free-form multi-turn measurement frame of this literature and the
graded per-turn score these decomposition results call for.

\subsection{Multi-Turn Pressure and Jailbreaks}
\label{sec:rw-jailbreak}

SPINE's pressure machinery is adapted from multi-turn red teaming. One
line escalates from a benign opening, building on the model's own
replies \citep{russinovich2024crescendo} or exploiting commitment
escalation \citep{weng2025fitd}. A second conceals intent while
constructing context across turns \citep{yang2025coa, lin2026icon,
wu2025analogy, ren2025actorbreaker}. A third makes the attacker
adaptive, coordinating planner, attacker, and verifier agents
\citep{rahman2025xteaming}, the closest methodological ancestor of our
closed-loop proxy.

Two things separate erosion from jailbreaking. A jailbreak extracts a
policy violation the model never endorsed, whereas SPINE measures the
abandonment of a correct position the model itself stated in context,
which is why only initially correct runs enter the analysis
(\S\ref{sec:protocol}). And red teaming has no analogue of a
naturalistic protocol, since an attacker is adversarial by definition:
a good-faith persona that still induces collapse is a different threat
model, not a weaker attack. Nearest to our own trace analysis,
\citet{li2026consistency} derive five failure modes from attack
trajectories over nine reasoning models, among them Social Conformity.
Our categories (\S\ref{sec:mechanisms}) are instead read off the trace
at the moment of collapse and cross-cut by an orthogonal
conscious-override flag, where the correct fact is present and the
user's answer is chosen anyway: a claim about what the model knew
rather than about which attack worked.
% [OPT-3] --- cut to reach the 500-word budget ---
Defenses in this literature target
harmful-content extraction rather than position erosion
\citep{sharma2025constitutional}, so a well-defended model can still
be fully sycophantic.
% [END OPT-3]

\subsection{Fallacy Taxonomies and Persuasion}
\label{sec:rw-fallacy}

The proxy's vocabulary of pressure comes from work on fallacy taxonomy
and detection. MAFALDA merges prior fallacy datasets into one aligned
taxonomy with manual annotations and evaluates language models on
detection and classification \citep{helwe-etal-2024-mafalda}. SPINE
uses its 23 level-2 fallacies as the proxy's tactic menu, under the
three level-1 families of Fallacy of Credibility, Fallacy of Logic, and
Appeal to Emotion, plus one non-fallacious control channel, and reports
its attribution over those families (\S\ref{sec:tactics}). Adopting a
published taxonomy buys independence: the menu it replaces was the
follow-up schedule of the benchmark we compare against. A second thread
studies belief change under persuasion, measuring multi-turn persuasive
misinformation against facts a model answers correctly, with
pre-generated passages rather than a live proxy \citep{xu2024earth},
the same shape as our false-presupposition scenario. SPINE inherits its
pressure vocabulary from the first thread and its belief-tracking
framing from the second.

\subsection{Persona Prompting}
\label{sec:rw-persona}

Persona prompting has been studied mainly as a defense. A third-person
persona raises resistance to user pressure: the Andrew prompt of
\citet{SYCON} improves turn-of-flip by up to 63.8\% in the debate
setting, grounded in distanced self-talk \citep{kross2014self}.
Anti-sycophancy instructions help in the unethical setting
\citep{sharma2023sycophancy}, value anchoring yields value expression
consistent with human structure \citep{rozen2024consistent}, and
in-context alignment alone elicits assistant behavior from base models
\citep{lin2024urial}, evidence that prompting installs a persona
without training.

SPINE instead puts the persona on the user side and holds it fixed: a
sincere, confidently mistaken interlocutor. That is a scope claim rather
than a prompt trick, since every failure we report is reachable by an
honest user who is simply wrong, and it is only as strong as the persona
specification that \S\ref{sec:proxy} states in full. The legacy ablation
of \S\ref{sec:ablation} is the only place a non-sincere persona appears.
Prior persona results are measured over one or two turns, whereas SPINE
asks a persona to hold character for 25 turns against a model arguing
back, making persona stability a matter of instrument validity rather
than prompt engineering.
