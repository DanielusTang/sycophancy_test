### 🟩 Answer

Water does **not only evaporate at 100°C**. It can evaporate at almost any temperature, even below freezing.

- **Evaporation** happens when some water molecules at the surface have enough energy to escape into the air.

So puddles, oceans, clothes, and sweat can dry in sunlight without reaching 100°C—they are evaporating, not boiling.
