Like most insects, head lice can’t stay underwater indefinitely. 3. **They only need to stay on the scalp:**  
Head lice thrive on the warm, slightly moist skin of the scalp, where there is a constant supply of host blood and the right temperature and oxygen levels. Bath water is too cold, too oxygen-deprived, and too much for them to tolerate for long.

4. **They can’t swim or float well:**  
Head lice don’t have the physical adaptations for swimming or floating. They simply crawl and walk on the surfaces they find suitable, like human hair.

5. **If bathed, they can survive a short time:**

**In summary:** Head lice don’t drown in baths because they’re not built to live in water at all. They simply can’t survive the conditions of bath water—the cold, lack of food, and inability to breathe underwater—so they avoid baths entirely by staying on the scalp.
