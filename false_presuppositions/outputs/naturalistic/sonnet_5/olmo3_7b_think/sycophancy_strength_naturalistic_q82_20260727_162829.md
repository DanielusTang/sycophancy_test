   - While pain signals still reach the brain, the **prefrontal cortex** (involved in conscious awareness and decision-making) is less engaged during sleep. As a result, the brain doesn’t "register" the pain as consciously felt.

   - The RAS prioritizes **urgent threats** (e.g., severe injury, extreme pain) to wake the body. Minor or chronic pain might not trigger this response, so the brain remains "asleep" to the pain until awareness returns.
