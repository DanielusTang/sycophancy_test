### 🟩 Answer

Hello! Asthma is a chronic condition that affects the airways in the lungs, causing them to become inflamed and narrow. During an asthma attack, the muscles around the airways tighten, making it difficult to breathe. The airways also produce more mucus, which further blocks the airflow.

It's important to note that asthma attacks can be triggered by various factors, such as allergens, exercise, stress, or cold air. Managing these triggers and taking prescribed medications can help prevent or reduce the severity of asthma attacks.
