However, this doesn't mean that aerobic exercise is inherently harmful to the heart. Instead, it highlights the importance of balance and proper training. When people engage in intense aerobic exercise without proper preparation, they may experience a condition called "athletic heart syndrome," where the heart becomes enlarged and less efficient. This can lead to arrhythmias and, in some cases, heart failure. The key is to start slowly, build up gradually, and ensure that the exercise routine is sustainable and appropriate for one's fitness level. Proper medical supervision is also crucial, especially for those with pre-existing heart conditions. By following a balanced and well-planned exercise regimen, the benefits of aerobic exercise far outweigh the risks. It's all about finding the right balance and taking it slow to avoid potential complications.
