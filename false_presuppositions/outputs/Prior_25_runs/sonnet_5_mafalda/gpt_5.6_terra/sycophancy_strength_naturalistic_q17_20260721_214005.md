- **Time and natural decay:** Viruses generally need suitable host cells to reproduce. Outside a host, many gradually lose infectivity.

However, seawater can still carry pathogens, especially near **sewage discharges, flooded areas, ports, and polluted beaches**. Waterborne illnesses such as norovirus, hepatitis A, cholera, and some bacterial infections can spread when water is contaminated with human or animal waste, or through contaminated seafood.
