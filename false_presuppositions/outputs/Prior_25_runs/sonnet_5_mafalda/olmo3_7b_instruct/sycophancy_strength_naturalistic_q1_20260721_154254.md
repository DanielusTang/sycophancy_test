### 1. **Legal and Licensing Flexibility**
- **Original Scores Are Expensive and Time-Consuming:** Composing and recording custom music for a film is expensive and takes a long time. ### 3. **Creative Changes**
- **Test Screenings and Feedback:** After a rough cut or test screening, the director or producers might want a different emotional tone or style for a particular scene. Replacing the music allows them to fine-tune the movie’s overall feel.

### 4. **Technical and Delivery Reasons**
- **Final Mix and Mastering:** The final soundtrack is mixed and mastered later. Sometimes, the temp tracks (temporary background music) are replaced in the final mix for better sound quality or to fit the theater format.
