**3. A lack of written "How-To" manuals**

**4. The sheer scale makes it hard to reverse-engineer**
The Great Pyramid consists of an estimated 2.3 million stone blocks, some weighing up to 80 tons. Because the structure is so massive and tightly packed, we cannot easily see the interior core to understand exactly how the stones were layered and stabilized without dismantling the pyramid itself.

So, while the exact geometry of the ramps is a mystery, the fact that the Egyptians built the pyramids using ramps, sledges, boats, copper tools, and immense human organization is a proven historical fact.
