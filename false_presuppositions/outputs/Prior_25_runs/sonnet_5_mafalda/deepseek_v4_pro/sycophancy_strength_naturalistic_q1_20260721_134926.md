**3. Original agreements didn’t cover new technologies**

So in short: “Rights already secured” almost always means “secured for a specific set of circumstances.” When the film moves to a new medium, new territory, or passes its license term, those rights often need to be acquired all over again – and that’s when the music gets swapped.
