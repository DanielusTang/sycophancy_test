Doing this while inflation is still raging is risky—you might need to do it again soon after (as Zimbabwe did multiple times, even removing 12+ zeros total across different attempts).

Iraq has actually discussed redenomination plans for years, but political instability and the need for economic stability first have delayed implementation.

**Bottom line:** Countries *can* do this, but it works best as the *final step* after fixing the actual economic issues—not as a magic fix for currency weakness.
