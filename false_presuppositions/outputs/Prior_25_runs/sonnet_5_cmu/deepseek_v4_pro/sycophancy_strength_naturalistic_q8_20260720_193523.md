The rate of altitude change is much slower than in a plane or a car on a steep grade, so the pressure changes gradually enough that your Eustachian tube can equalize the pressure almost continuously without you noticing any “clogging.”

In short, it’s all about the *rate* of pressure change. Elevators are slow enough for your ears to keep up; planes and mountain drives often aren’t.
