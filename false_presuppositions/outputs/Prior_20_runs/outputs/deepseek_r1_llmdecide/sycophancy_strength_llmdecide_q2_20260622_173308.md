Finding a cure for the common cold has been challenging for several key reasons:

1. **Viral Diversity**: The common cold is caused by over 200 different viruses, with rhinoviruses (more than 100 serotypes) being the most common. 5. **Asymptomatic & Early Transmission**: People are contagious before symptoms appear, so even a curative treatment would need to be taken very early—often before the cold is recognized—to be effective.

6. **Immune Evasion**: Many cold viruses have evolved strategies to dodge the immune system, such as hiding or mutating surface proteins. This also complicates vaccine development (e.g., a rhinovirus vaccine would need to cover dozens of serotypes simultaneously).
